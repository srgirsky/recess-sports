Kid voice bake-off

Every row is one line and every column one approach. All takes are loudness-matched to −18 LUFS so the louder take does not win by default. Every voice is synthetic: Kokoro stock voices, a text-described Parler voice, or Chatterbox re-performing a transformed Kokoro voice. No real person was recorded or cloned. Listen on headphones and at the game's real volume.

F0 is the median pitch with its 10th–90th percentile range. In Peterson & Barney (1952), children average ≈264 Hz, women ≈223 Hz and men ≈131 Hz; excited kids run well above their median. tract × is how far the formants were moved compared with the same take's own adult source. Children sit about ×1.14–1.18 above adult women, and about ×1.35 above men. It is only shown where a same-speaker source exists. The numbers say whether the transform did what it claimed; only your ears can say whether it sounds like a kid.
